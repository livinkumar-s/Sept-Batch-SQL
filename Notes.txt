BETWEEN  
----------
inclusive range 
Ex: WHERE col BETWEEN n1 AND n2;  

IN, NOT IN  
------------
Ex: WHERE col IN (v1,v2.......);

LIKE, NOT LIKE
----------------
Pattern Matching 
Ex: WHERE StrCol LIKE Pattern;

Wildcards(pattern)
--------------------
% --> matches 0 to inf no of chars.
_ --> only 1 char.

"a%" --> "anand", "anshiya" "a"
"%a"  --> "anshiya" "a" "leo"

"_a_" --> "don" "dan"
